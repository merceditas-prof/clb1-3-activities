Greetings and times of day

In English we have four greetings for different times. We also have hello and hi for any time.

We sayWhenHello or goodbye?
Good morning.5:00 a.m. - 11:59 a.m.Hello
Good afternoon.12:00 p.m. - 5:59 p.m.Hello
Good evening.6:00 p.m. - 11:59 p.m.Hello
Hello. / Hi.Any timeHello
Good night.Evening or nightGoodbye

Careful! Good night is not hello. It is a goodbye. We say good night when we go to bed, or when we will not see the person again until tomorrow.

In many languages, "good night" means "good evening". In English it is different.

Formal or casual?

We change our words for different people. Formal is for work, school, a boss, or a person we do not know. Casual (informal) is for friends and family.

Formal: Good morning. Good afternoon. Good evening. Goodbye. Have a good day. See you next week.

Casual: Hi! Hey! How's it going? What's up? Bye. Bye-bye. See ya! Later.

Not sure? Hello and goodbye are safe with everybody.

How is ... ? and How was ... ?

We can ask about many things, not only about the person.

NOW (present) = How is your family? How is your work? How is your school? How is your new job? How is your new house?

FINISHED (past) = How was your day? How was your weekend? How was your holiday?

Nice to meet you, or nice to see you?

Nice to meet you. = the first time only.

Nice to see you. = the second time, the third time, every day.

Long time no see! = we did not see the person for a long time.

The conversation

In English a short conversation has the same order every time. Follow the eight steps.

1

You say a greeting.

Hello.

Also: Hi. / Good morning. / Good afternoon. / Good evening.

2

The other person answers.

Good morning.

Also: Hello. / Hi.

3

You ask about the other person.

How are you?

Also: How are you doing?

4

The other person answers and asks you back.

I'm fine, thanks. And you?

Also: I'm good, thank you. And you? / Not bad. And you?

5

You ask a how question.

How is your job?

Also: How is your family? / How was your weekend?

6

The other person answers.

Very good.

Also: Good. / So-so. / Not bad.

7

You finish the conversation.

See you later.

Also: Goodbye. / See you tomorrow. / Have a good day.

8

The other person answers.

Bye!

Also: Bye-bye. / Goodbye. / See you!

Two important things.

1. Answer the question. Do not repeat your partner's words.
Your partner says: "Hello, my name is Kate. What is your name?"
You say: "Hello, my name is Maria." (Not: "My name is Kate.")

2. Always ask back. Say "And you?"
"How are you?" - "I'm good, thank you. And you?"
If you do not ask back, it is not polite.

Example 1: formal (at work or at school)

A: Good morning.

B: Good morning.

A: How are you?

B: I'm fine, thank you. And you?

A: Very good, thank you. How is your work?

B: Not bad. How was your weekend?

A: It was great, thank you. Have a good day.

B: Thank you. Goodbye.

Example 2: casual (with a friend)

A: Hi!

B: Hey! How's it going?

A: Good, thanks. And you?

B: Not bad. How is your family?

A: Very good, thanks. How was your holiday?

B: So-so.

A: Okay, gotta go. See ya!

B: Bye-bye!
