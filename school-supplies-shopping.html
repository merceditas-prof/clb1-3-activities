School Supplies Shopping

Look at the baskets, write a shopping list  •  Read, listen, and answer

How to Use This Page

  1. Look at the pictures in Basket 1.
  2. On your own paper, write a shopping list of every item you see.
  3. Use the Word Bank below if you need help with spelling.
  4. Do the same for Basket 2, Basket 3, and Basket 4.

Tip: Write "I need..." before your list. Example: I need a backpack.

How to Play

Tip: Read the conversation two times before you answer.
