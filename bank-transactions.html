Bank Transactions

EEE 2-3 (CLB 2-3) | transaction words, word forms, debits and credits, polite requests at the bank | class hub + practice games

Day 1: Bank Transaction Words (1 h 45)

1. The picture (8 min). Look at the bank picture together. Point to the teller, the bank machine, the cheque, the cash. Yes or No.
2. Transaction words (20 min). Listen and repeat (Audio tab, Day 1, Track 1), match, write the word under each picture, then spelling (Track 2). Then the Transaction words game.VIDEOBanking vocabulary (ESL Directions, CLB 1-3, 2 min)
Section 2. Play once, then again and pause on each word: the class repeats.
VIDEOUSED BEFOREBanking Vocabulary for beginner ESL (2 min)
Section 2, extra. Students write every transaction word they hear.
GAMEWordwall - At the bank (flash cards)
Section 2 or wrap-up. Share your screen; students say the word before you turn the card.
3. Money in or money out? (14 min). Read the two boxes. IN or OUT, then a or b. Then the Money in or out? game.
4. Amira's busy week (16 min). Listen (Track 3) and number the transactions. Then She needs to ... with the word box.
5. Spell and read (16 min). Unscramble the words. Leo's story (Track 4), then True or False. Then the What does it mean? game.GAMEWordwall - Banking quiz (deposit, withdraw, e-Transfer ...)
Section 5, extra. Whole-class quiz on your screen; name a student for each question.
6. Communicate (23 min). Model (Track 5). Pairs in the main room, then breakout rooms: What do you do at your bank?
Homework: the Day 1 practice form (auto-graded) - open the form - plus any game on this page.

Day 2: Word Forms, Debits and Credits (1 h 45)

1. Review (10 min). Write the transaction under each picture. Spelling (Audio tab, Day 2, Track 2).
2. Verb or noun? (20 min). The table (Track 1): read and repeat. V or N, then circle the word. Then the Verb or noun? game.
3. -s and -ed (14 min). Read the box. Fill in with the word box, then the plurals. Then the -s or -ed? game.
4. Rosa's bank (15 min). Listen and read (Track 3). True or False, then the questions.
5. Debit or credit? (20 min). Debit = money out, credit = money in. D or C, then Rosa's statement. Then the Debit or credit? and Rosa's statement games.READINGMoney Mentors - Reading a Bank Statement (Canadian, 1 page PDF)
Section 5, extra. A second statement: find the deposits, the withdrawals and the balance. Pre-teach CHQ and W/D.
GAMELearn English Feel Good - At the bank 1 (English for new immigrants)
Section 2 or 5, extra. Do it together on your screen (uses US 'checking').
6. Communicate (18 min). Model (Track 4). Write 5 sentences about your bank, then read them in breakout rooms.
Homework: the Day 2 practice form (auto-graded) - open the form - plus any game on this page.

Day 3: Polite Requests at the Bank (1 h 45)

1. Who is polite? (8 min). The picture, then a or b: which sentence is more polite? Then the Which is more polite? game.
2. Could you please ...? (20 min). The table. Listen and repeat (Audio tab, Day 3, Track 1). Make it polite, word order, Polite or Order?VIDEOengVid - How to make polite requests (7 min)
Section 2, a short clip only: the Could you please ...? examples. Students repeat.
3. Could I ...? / I'd like to ... (24 min). Listen and repeat (Track 2). Which is correct? Fill in, correct the mistake, what do you say? Then the Which is correct? and What do you say? games.GAMEWordwall - Forming 'I would like' statements (ESL Directions)
Section 3, extra. Preview it first; do it together on your screen.
4. At the teller (20 min). The dialogue (Track 3), fill in. Four short conversations (Tracks 4 to 7).VIDEOAt a bank - four short teller dialogues (3 min)
Section 4. Pause after each dialogue: What does the customer want? (US spelling 'check').
GAMEWordwall - Opening a bank account (beginners gap fill)
Section 4, extra. Complete the dialogue together.
5. Communicate (25 min). Role play at the teller in breakout rooms: customer, teller, observer - then change roles.VIDEOOpening a bank account (ESL Directions, CLB 1-3, 1.5 min)
Section 5, before the role play. Ask: What does the customer say? I would like to ...
Homework: the Day 3 practice form (auto-graded) - open the form - plus any game on this page.
