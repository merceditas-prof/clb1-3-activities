Lesson 1: Log in to Avenue

New words: log in password dashboard course announcements

  1. Open Chrome or Safari. Type avenue.ca. Click Log In.
  2. Type your email and password. Click Log In.

Forgot your password? Click Forgot your password?

  1. You see the Dashboard. Click YCDSB – 2026/2027 CLB 2-3.
  1. First time only: a box says "Welcome to the Student Course Tour". Click Skip tour.
  1. Look at General. Announcements = homework. BigBlueButton Virtual Classroom = our class room.
  1. Click Announcements. Read it every day after class.
  1. Click your picture (top right) → Profile. Is your full name there? No? Tell the teacher.

Save avenue.ca in your bookmarks (computer) or on your home screen (phone).

Lesson 2: Join the class room

New words: join microphone allow muted

  1. Log in. Open our class. In General, click BigBlueButton Virtual Classroom.
  2. Click Join session. A new tab opens.
  1. The browser asks to use your microphone. Click Allow.
  2. "Your audio settings": click Join audio.
  1. You come in muted. That is OK. Your name is on the left.

On a phone: keep your screen ON. If the screen turns off, you leave the class. Don't open other apps.

Lesson 4: Breakout rooms

New words: breakout room timer main room

  1. The teacher opens the rooms. A box says "Join breakout room". Click Join room. A new tab opens.
  1. The blue bar at the top = time left.
  1. Time ends: "The breakout has ended". Click OK. You are back with the class.

Closed the box by mistake? Click Breakout Rooms on the left. Join again.
