Close to / Near / Far from

Close to and near mean the same thing. Far from is the opposite.

  • I live close to the library. = I live near the library.
  • I live far from the fire station.
  • The school is near the community centre.
  • The pharmacy is far from the bus stop.
  • My apartment is close to the grocery store.
  • The gas station is far from the police station.
  • The post office is near the shopping mall.
  • The hospital is far from the park.

Quick note: use "a" before a consonant sound (a bank, a school). Use "an" before a vowel sound (an office, an apartment building, an ATM).
