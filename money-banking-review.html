Money and Banking - Review

EEE 2-3 (CLB 2-3) | Canadian money, banks and bank accounts, bank statement words | class hub + practice games

Day 1: Canadian Money (1 h 45)

1. The picture and the menu (10 min). Look at the cafe picture. Point to the cashier, the customer, the cash register, the menu. Ask: How much is a coffee? How much is the lunch special?
2. Coins and bills (8 min). Fast review in the unit document, then the spelling dictation (Audio tab, Track 1).VIDEOUSED BEFORETVOKids - Our Canadian Coins
Section 2. Play a short part, pause and ask: What coin is this? How much is it?
SITEUSED BEFOREBank of Canada - the bills
Section 2. Real pictures of the $5, $10, $20, $50 and $100 bills. Ask: What colour is it?
3. 13 or 30? (25 min). Listen and repeat, listen and circle, numbers in sentences, price dictation (Audio tab, Tracks 2 to 5). Then play the 13 or 30? game together.LISTENINGUSED BEFOREAgendaweb - Numbers: -teen or -ty
Section 3, extra. Play each audio; students write the number they hear in their notebook.
GAMEUSED BEFOREMy class page: Going Shopping - the -teen / -ty game
Section 3, extra. Open the -teen / -ty tab: the page says a number, students choose.
VIDEOUSED BEFOREPrices in Dollars and Cents (4 min)
Section 3 or 6. Pause after each price; students say it, then write it.
4. Make change (18 min). Count the money in the document, the change dialogue (Track 6), then the Count the money and Your change games.
5. Count or non-count? (15 min). money, cash and change have no -s. Then How much or How many. Two quick games in Day 1 Games.
6. Listening: How much is it? (10 min). Four short conversations (Audio tab, Tracks 7 to 10). Play each one two times.LISTENINGUSED BEFOREPractice listening to prices (1 min)
Section 6, extra. A very short price dictation. Play it twice.
LISTENINGUSED BEFOREEnglish-Practice - A1 Prices (5 short listenings)
Section 6, extra. Pick one audio; students write the prices they hear.
GAMEUSED BEFOREMy class page: School Supplies - Prices
Extra. Price dictation and matching games from the school supplies unit.
7. Communicate (12 min). Breakout rooms: at the Sunrise Cafe. Customer and cashier; in a group of three, the third student is the observer.
Homework: the Day 1 practice form (auto-graded) - open the form - plus any game on this page.

Day 2: Banks and Bank Accounts (1 h 45)

1. The picture (8 min). Look at the bank picture. Point to the customer, the representative, the bank card, the teller, the bank machine. Yes or No together.
2. Bank words (16 min). Listen and repeat (Audio tab, Day 2, Track 1), match and fill in, then the spelling dictation (Track 2). Then the Bank words game.VIDEOUSED BEFOREBanking Vocabulary for beginner ESL (2 min)
Section 2. Play it once with no pause, then again: pause on each word and the class repeats.
VIDEOUSED BEFOREBeginner English Vocabulary - Banking Information (3 min)
Section 2, extra. Some American words (checking = chequing). Students write 5 words they hear.
GAMEUSED BEFORELiveworksheets - Parts of a Bank Card
Section 2, extra. Label a bank card together on your screen; students say the answers.
3. Chequing, savings or joint? (17 min). Read the three accounts, write C, S or J, then compare the two statements. Then the Which account? game.
4. Opening an account (11 min). Farah's story (Track 3): read it together, then True or False.VIDEOUSED BEFOREellii - Going to the Bank: Everyday English Dialogues (2 min)
Section 4 or 7. A customer opens a chequing account. Play it before the role play; ask: What does she want?
5. Account plans (14 min). The Maple Bank brochure: True or False, then Which plan is good for ...? Then the Account plans game.READINGUSED BEFORELiveworksheets - Comparing Bank Accounts (a little harder)
Section 5, extra. Do it together on your screen after the Maple Bank plans.
6. Interest (19 min). The bank pays you, or you pay the bank. Three words: interest, interested, interesting. Listening about Dina (Tracks 4 and 5). Two games.
7. Communicate (12 min). Breakout rooms: open a bank account (Track 6 is the model). In a group of three, the third student is the observer.
Homework: the Day 2 practice form (auto-graded) - open the form - plus any game on this page.

Day 3: Bank Statement Words and Review (1 h 45)

1. The picture (7 min). Look at the bank statement. What is it? Whose statement is it? Yes or No together.
2. Statement words (22 min). Listen and repeat (Audio tab, Day 3, Track 1), label the statement, match, fill in, spelling (Track 2). Then the Label the statement game.GAMEUSED BEFORELiveworksheets - Bank Statement Headings
Section 2, extra. Do it together on your screen: students say where each heading goes.
3. Money in or money out? (8 min). A quick sort in the document, then the game.
4. Listening and dictation (10 min). Omar's statement (Track 3) and the dictation (Track 4).
5. Review quiz (22 min). Two teams, four rounds; Round 2 is listening (Track 5). Finish with the Big review quiz game on this page.
6. Speaking day (28 min). Breakout rooms of three: one role play, three rounds, change roles every round (Track 6 is the model).VIDEOUSED BEFOREellii - Going to the Bank: Everyday English Dialogues (2 min)
Section 6. Play it before the role play as a second model.
Homework: the Day 3 practice form (auto-graded) - open the form - plus any game on this page.
