Going Shopping - Day 1: How Much Is It?

CLB 1-3 | Canadian money, prices and sizes | class hub + practice games

Class flow (1 h 45)

1. Warm-up + the picture (15 min). Look at the store picture above. What do you see? Point to: a salesperson, a customer, a price tag. Ask: How much is a jacket? A dress? The shoes?
2. Dollars and cents (25 min). Coins and bills in the unit document. Then play the Coins Game tab together, or watch: VIDEOTVOKids - Our Canadian Coins
Teacher Andrea presents nickel, dime, quarter, loonie, toonie. Play a segment, pause, ask "What coin is this? How much?"
3. -teen or -ty? (10 min). Use the -teen / -ty tab (the page speaks the numbers), or play the classic: LISTENINGAgendaweb - Numbers: -teen or -ty
Play each audio; students write the number they hear (dictation) or shout it out.
4. Sizes (10 min). S - M - L - XL chart in the unit document. Ask 3-4 students: "What size are you?"
5. Price tags (15 min). Do exercise 5A in the document, then the Price Tags tab for instant-check practice.
6. Reading + listening (12 min). Sara's Shopping Day in the document - the story and the dictations are recorded on the Audio tab, matched to the unit exercises. Extra listening practice: LISTENINGEnglish-Practice - A1 Prices (5 exercises)
Five short audios with tasks, transcripts and keys. Pick one; students write the prices they hear.
7. Communicate (13 min). Two store windows in the document (breakout rooms). Finish with the Dialogue tab as a whole-class game.
